How it works

Native OpenXR games run through KRVR's own OpenXR runtime on the PC, with no SteamVR in the chain. Everything else runs through SteamVR with the KRVR driver. The headset side is the same either way.

STEP 1

Install the server

Run the free KRVR installer on your Windows PC, or the test package for Linux and SteamOS. It sets up the OpenXR runtime, the SteamVR driver and the firewall for you.

STEP 2

Press Connect

Start the server on the PC, open KRVR on Vision Pro and tap Connect. The headset finds your PC on the local network by itself.

STEP 3

Launch your game

Start any VR game on the PC, or launch it from the games panel in the headset. It appears at full detail where you are looking, with sound, microphone and controllers.

Features

Picture and smoothness

Eye-tracked foveated streaming

The full-detail region follows your gaze, so the bandwidth goes where you are looking and the picture stays sharp where it matters. Gaze data stays on your own network.

Steady frame delivery

Every frame is locked to the headset display. Motion Smoothing holds a game that cannot keep up to an even, steady rate instead of a ragged one.

90 or 120 Hz

On Vision Pro with M5, choose 90 or 120 Hz in the headset and the game runs at the rate you chose. 120 Hz runs in Mixed immersion.

Play your way

PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers

Full tracking, buttons, finger touch and haptics in your PC games, through both OpenXR and SteamVR.

Hand and eye tracking

Vision Pro tracks your hands and your eyes, and KRVR passes both to games that support them. Play VRChat and X-Plane with your bare hands.

Audio and microphone

Game audio to the headset or your PC speakers. For your headset mic in games, add the free VB-CABLE on the PC. How to set it up.

Your space, your controls

Cockpit masks and immersion

Cut a passthrough window into the game to see your wheel, rig or keyboard. Choose Full, Mixed or Progressive immersion, with the Digital Crown fading your room in and out.

Games and desktop in the headset

Start and stop your PC games from the games panel, and see your PC screen in the headset for launchers and settings, on any monitor including ultrawides.

Live tuning in the pill

Bitrate, fovea size, sharpening, FOV crop and refresh rate change while you play, from a small pill you can move or hide. No restarts.

Works with

Played on KRVR by its users. Any OpenXR or SteamVR game should work.

Requirements

Windows PC

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • NVIDIA or AMD GPU
  • Latest GPU drivers
  • Wired ethernet to your router
  • SteamVR installed for SteamVR games

Linux or SteamOS PC (testing)

  • Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, Fedora 39 or newer, Arch, or SteamOS
  • NVIDIA or AMD GPU
  • SteamVR installed for SteamVR games
  • Download the test version

Apple Vision Pro

  • KRVR from the App Store
  • Same local network as the PC
  • 5 GHz Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi 6 recommended
  • 120 Hz needs Vision Pro with M5

Good to have

  • A dedicated Wi-Fi 6 access point near where you play
  • PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers
  • VB-CABLE on the PC for your headset microphone

Quick fixes

  • Headset cannot find the PC — Both must be on the same network. Check the PC app is started, pick the right network adapter in its IP list, and allow KRVR through Windows Firewall.
  • Game opens on the monitor — Connect before launching. For OpenXR games set the display mode to OpenXR in the game's settings. SteamVR games open SteamVR, which now shows KRVR as the headset.
  • Installer says files are still the old version — SteamVR or a game was holding them. Close them, run the installer again.
